Paying for child care can put real pressure on a family budget. If cost is keeping you from considering licensed care, Child Action is a useful place to start.

This guide explains the process in plain language. Assistance programs have different rules, and those rules can change, so use it as a starting point and confirm your situation directly with Child Action.

## What Child Action does

Child Action, Inc. is Sacramento County's child care resource and referral agency. It helps families find care and administers several programs that can help eligible families pay for it.

The important word is **several**. Sacramento County families may receive help through CalWORKs child care, an Alternative Payment Program, Head Start, State Preschool, or another program. Each has its own eligibility requirements and enrollment process.

That is why there is no single income number or checklist that accurately answers whether every family qualifies.

## Start with the Child Care Eligibility List

The **Child Care Eligibility List**, usually called the CEL, connects one family application with multiple subsidized programs in Sacramento County.

Joining the CEL increases your chances of receiving assistance, but it does not guarantee funding or an immediate opening. Some programs also maintain their own enrollment or waiting lists outside the CEL.

You can [apply through Child Action's official assistance page](https://childaction.org/apply-for-subsidized-care/) or call **(916) 369-0191** to complete the application with a representative.

If you receive CalWORKs cash aid, recently stopped receiving it, or participate in Welfare-to-Work, contact your CalWORKs worker as well. Your route to assistance may be different from the standard CEL process.

## What eligibility depends on

The details vary by program, but Child Action may ask about:

- Household income and family size
- Your child's age
- Why care is needed, such as work, school, training, or an approved activity
- Your current or recent CalWORKs participation
- The type and schedule of care you need

Avoid relying on an income percentage copied from an older article or flyer. Child Action's current eligibility tools and representatives are the right source for the rule that applies to your family.

## Can you choose your provider?

It depends on the assistance program.

Alternative Payment Program certificates generally allow families to choose an eligible licensed provider, family child care home, or qualifying license-exempt provider. Other programs enroll children through specific sites or networks.

T.L.C. Footprints accepts Child Action assistance. Child Action can confirm whether your approval may be used here, what portion it covers, and whether your family will have a fee.

## Keep your information current

Child Action recommends updating your CEL information every six months, or sooner if your income, address, or family size changes.

Current contact information matters. If a program cannot reach you, you may miss an opportunity for care. Set a reminder when you apply and log in periodically to check your application status.

## What happens after you apply

Many families are placed on a waiting list because funding is limited. Some are contacted quickly when they meet the requirements for an available program; others wait longer.

While you wait:

1. Keep your CEL information current.
2. Check your application status through Child Action.
3. Compare providers that accept the type of assistance you may receive.
4. Ask each provider about availability for your child's age and schedule.
