## What changes the total cost

### Enrollment deposit

T.L.C. Footprints requires a **$150 non-refundable enrollment deposit** when you reserve a space.

When comparing programs, ask how much the deposit is, when it is due, whether it is refundable, and whether it is applied to tuition.

### Enrollment rather than attendance

At T.L.C. Footprints, tuition reserves your child's space. The rate remains the same when your child is absent because of illness, vacation, or another family choice.

Ask every provider how absences, holidays, provider closures, vacations, and schedule changes affect tuition. Similar weekly rates can produce very different annual costs when those policies differ.

### Meals and snacks

T.L.C. Footprints provides breakfast, a morning snack, and an afternoon snack. Families provide lunch.

Ask what food is included, what must be sent from home, and how allergies or dietary needs are handled.

### Diapers, wipes, and other supplies

Providers have different supply policies. Ask who provides diapers, wipes, sunscreen, bedding, activity materials, and replacement clothing.

### Closures and holidays

Request the annual closure calendar and ask which days are paid. Knowing the dates helps you calculate cost and arrange backup care.

### Late pickup and schedule changes

Confirm the late-pickup policy, when a fee begins, and how temporary or permanent schedule changes are handled.

## What “part-time” means

Part-time can refer to fewer days, shorter days, or a specific schedule selected from the provider's available openings. Do not assume two programs define it the same way.

Ask:

1. Which days and hours are available?
2. Is the schedule fixed from week to week?
3. Can days be exchanged or added?
4. How much notice is required for a schedule change?

## Four questions that make rates comparable

When you contact a provider, write down the answers to these four questions:

1. What is the weekly rate for my child's age and schedule?
2. What deposit or registration fees are required?
3. What is included, and what does my family provide?
4. Which absences, holidays, and closure days are paid?

Those answers will tell you more than the advertised weekly rate alone.

## Help paying for child care

Sacramento County families may qualify for assistance through one of several programs connected with Child Action. Eligibility and funding vary, so check the current requirements rather than relying on an old income threshold.

T.L.C. Footprints accepts Child Action assistance. Read the guide to [applying for child care assistance in Sacramento County](/resources/child-action-subsidy-sacramento) and confirm your individual eligibility with Child Action.
